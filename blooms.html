Welcome to Bloom's Taxonomy

Click on any level of the pyramid above to explore the six levels of cognitive learning. Each level builds upon the previous one, creating a hierarchy of thinking skills from basic recall to creative synthesis.

Start with "Remember" at the base and work your way up to "Create" at the top!

1. REMEMBER

The foundation of learning - recalling facts, basic concepts, and answers. This involves retrieving relevant knowledge from long-term memory.

Key Action Verbs:

Define List Recall Identify Name State Describe Match

Examples:

  • List the planets in our solar system
  • Define photosynthesis
  • Name the capital cities of European countries
  • Recall the multiplication tables

2. UNDERSTAND

Constructing meaning from messages, including oral, written, and graphic communication. This involves interpreting, exemplifying, classifying, summarizing, and explaining.

Key Action Verbs:

Explain Interpret Summarize Paraphrase Classify Compare Contrast Illustrate

Examples:

  • Explain how photosynthesis works in your own words
  • Summarize the main themes of a novel
  • Compare and contrast democracy and monarchy
  • Interpret data from a graph or chart

3. APPLY

Using knowledge and skills in new situations. This involves executing or implementing procedures in familiar or unfamiliar contexts.

Key Action Verbs:

Apply Execute Implement Solve Use Demonstrate Calculate Construct

Examples:

  • Use the Pythagorean theorem to solve real-world problems
  • Apply grammar rules when writing an essay
  • Implement a scientific method in an experiment
  • Calculate compound interest on a loan

4. ANALYZE

Breaking material into constituent parts and detecting relationships between parts and overall structure or purpose.

Key Action Verbs:

Analyze Break down Compare Contrast Examine Categorize Differentiate Investigate

Examples:

  • Analyze the causes of World War I
  • Break down a complex math problem into steps
  • Examine the relationship between characters in a story
  • Compare different marketing strategies and their effectiveness

5. EVALUATE

Making judgments based on criteria and standards. This involves checking and critiquing based on internal evidence or external criteria.

Key Action Verbs:

Evaluate Judge Critique Assess Rate Justify Defend Support

Examples:

  • Evaluate the effectiveness of different teaching methods
  • Judge the credibility of news sources
  • Critique a scientific research study
  • Assess the environmental impact of different policies

6. CREATE

Putting elements together to form a coherent whole or reorganizing into a new pattern. This is the highest level involving generating, planning, and producing.

Key Action Verbs:

Create Design Generate Plan Produce Invent Compose Develop

Examples:

  • Design a new app to solve a social problem
  • Create an original piece of art or music
  • Develop a business plan for a startup
  • Compose a research proposal for a scientific study
