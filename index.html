Welcome

The Path from Practitioner to Manager — and Beyond

This course library contains the complete set of interactive lessons for the NCyTE Certified Information Security Manager (CISM) program, plus a two-part bridge module connecting CISM mastery to ISACA's emerging AAISM (Advanced in AI Security Management) credential. CISM is ISACA's flagship credential for security managers, focused on the four domains of governance, risk management, program development, and incident management. The certification is widely recognized as the management complement to technical credentials like CISSP — it tests your ability to lead a security program, not just operate one.

Each lesson is a single-page interactive experience: scenario-driven, lab-style exercises, embedded calculators, knowledge checks, and a final 10-question assessment. Open one and work straight through, or use the sticky navigation inside each lesson to revisit specific modules.

Course Complete — All 8 Core Lessons Plus 2 Bridge Lessons Available

The full four-domain CISM course is published, along with a two-part bridge module covering AI-specific governance, risk, threats, controls, and incident response. You can begin at the start with Domain 2.1 (the foundational risk vocabulary lesson) or jump directly to any lesson you want to focus on.

A Note on How This Course Is Built

Each lesson follows the same pattern — a strategic concept anchored by a memorable analogy, expanded with examples drawn from real incidents, and reinforced with hands-on interactive exercises. The goal isn't to memorize ISACA's vocabulary; it's to build the judgment that a CISM-certified manager uses every day. The exam tests vocabulary. The job tests judgment. We aim for both.

How to Use the Lessons

  • Self-paced study — open a lesson, work through every module in order, end with the final quiz. Plan on a single lesson per study session.
  • Classroom delivery — instructors project the lesson and lead the class through interactive exercises together. Each module includes discussion prompts.
  • Reference — once worked through, the sticky module navigation makes it easy to return to a specific concept (BIA, CVSS, lifecycle, OWASP LLM Top 10, etc.) without re-reading the lesson.
  • Exam prep — work through every lesson's final quiz; lessons where you score below 8/10 are the ones to revisit.
  • Bridge progression — after completing the four CISM domains, the Bridge Module extends your toolkit to AI security; take Part 1 before Part 2.
A Word About the CISM Exam

ISACA's CISM exam is 150 questions, four hours, and four domains weighted approximately: Governance (17%), Risk Management (20%), Program Development (33%), Incident Management (30%). This course covers all four. The two largest domains — Risk and Program Development — are the highest leverage for exam prep time. The two domains with the most "scenario judgment" questions — Risk and Incident Management — are where strong reading comprehension and the priority frameworks taught here will earn the most points.

A Word About the AAISM Bridge

ISACA's AAISM (Advanced in AI Security Management) is an emerging credential for CISM-certified leaders extending their practice into AI security. The bridge module is not a substitute for AAISM exam preparation, but it covers the conceptual territory: AI governance frameworks (NIST AI RMF, EU AI Act, ISO/IEC 42001), AI risk taxonomy, OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications, MITRE ATLAS, AIBOM, AI red-teaming, and adapted incident response. The structure carries from CISM. The contents are new.
