Course Overview

Welcome to Network Essentials/Network+ - Fall 2025

Course Description

This course introduces the operation of computer networks and networking devices. The course provides an examination of the evolution of data communications and computer networks. Hardware and software network configurations, operations, and requirements will be discussed. Topics include network media, physical and logical topologies, the OSI model, protocols and standards, network implementation, and network support. This course is designed to prepare students for the Network+ certification.

  3. Participate in the information systems equipment procurement, testing, deployment, operations, maintenance and dispensation of equipment

Course Learning Objectives

  1. Describe history and evolution of data communications and computer networks
  2. Determine hardware and software requirements to operate various types of networks
  3. Describe the various types of data transmission media topologies
  4. Describe Ethernet media access
  5. Categorize network operations in terms of the OSI model
  6. Discuss various types of protocol stacks and their advantages and disadvantages
  7. Analyze various types of network configurations
  8. Use various TCP/IP utilities to troubleshoot and analyze IP networks
  9. Describe the purpose and characteristics of fault tolerance
